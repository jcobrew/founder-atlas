# 0rbital Product Scope

0rbital (pronounced **Orbital**) currently includes **only co-living founder programs**: places where builders live and build together for a fixed term.

## Current launch scope

A program is in scope for the active 0rbital dataset only when it is a residential builder cohort, such as:

- **Co-living founder residencies** — live-in or relocation-based cohorts for founders and builders.
- **Hacker houses / founder houses** — coliving organized around a builder, hacker, or startup scene.
- **Live-in builder cohorts** — programs where housing or residential participation is core to the experience.

In data terms, active records should generally use `canonicalType: founder-residency`, `canonicalType: hacker-house`, or `format: live-in`.

## Excluded from the active dataset

Do **not** add the following categories to the active launch dataset during the current scope:

- Generic accelerators.
- Government grants.
- Startup visas.
- Cofounder matching.
- Non-residential fellowships.
- Generic remote-worker or nomad coliving.
- Paid founder networks without residential cohorts.
- Startup campuses for now.

These categories may be useful to founders, but they would broaden 0rbital beyond the current co-living product promise.

## Future note

Startup campuses, paid founder networks, and broader builder environments may become a separate category or product surface later. Until that future scope is explicitly defined, approved, and implemented, they should **not** be re-added to the active dataset for the current launch.
